Q-Life Approach

Holistic care delivered wholeheartedly through the end of life.

Treatment for cancer sufferers is carried out comprehensively: overcoming inflammation, eliminating pain, repairing cell damage that occurs, improving the patient's quality of life holistically.

We apply the same approach to stroke, diabetes mellitus with its complications, auto-immune diseases, kidney failure, dementia, Alzheimer's, and other critical illnesses that are in the final stages.

Five dimensions of care

We care for the whole person holistically.

Quality of life is maintained from five dimensions at once: it is not just physical complaints that are treated.

Dimension 01

Bio

Physical symptoms are relieved: pain, shortness of breath, nausea, and treatment side effects are carefully managed.

Dimension 02

Psycho

Emotional well-being is looked after: anxiety and depression get space to talk about.

Dimension 03

Socio

Families are involved and supported: communication, decision making and mentoring work together.

Dimension 04

Spiritual

Meaning, hope, and inner peace are respected according to each patient's beliefs.

Dimension 05

Culture

Cultural values, beliefs, customs, habits and way of life of patients and their families that need to be understood and respected during the care process.
