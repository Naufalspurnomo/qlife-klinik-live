2004
Establishment

Q-Life Klinik was founded on April 19 2004. Previously located on Jalan Raya Jemursari 335, Surabaya, then developed in Ruko Villa Bukit Mas RE-8, Jl. Abdul Wahab Siamin, Dukuh Pakis, Surabaya.

NOW
Scope of treatment

As a center for complementary therapy, palliative cancer, and Ozone therapy for chronic diseases, Q-Life Klinik serves patients with end-stage cancer and chronic conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, asthma, auto immune disease, prostate, and impotence.
